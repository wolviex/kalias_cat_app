# Project background

## Why

Kalia & The Feline Friends: Calm & Clever is an educational life-sim / Tamagotchi-style app for children ages 3–8. It combines caring for companion cats with calm, age-appropriate learning activities so emotional self-care is presented alongside early literacy, maths, and logic.

## Goals

- Make a nurturing room where care actions, emotional states, and learning games reinforce each other.
- Adapt complexity, pacing, controls, and content across Explorer, Navigator, and Master difficulty tiers.
- Reward care and learning through the Purr-gress and Magical Trunk loop without monetized advantage.
- Deliver Android first, with Flutter Web supporting review and iteration.

## Non-goals

- Ads, third-party data collection, pay-to-win progression, harsh feedback, and failure states for Explorer.
- iOS distribution, cloud sync, parent dashboards, and purchases in the current release scope.

## Target user

Children aged 3–8 are the players; parents choose the difficulty tier and are the eventual account and release stakeholders. Educational and content collaborators provide age-appropriate learning material and media.
