## Decision

The app is a calm educational life-sim for ages 3–8. It uses parent-selected difficulty tiers, gentle feedback, and a care-and-learning reward loop; it must not include ads, pay-to-win mechanics, harsh feedback, or fail states for the youngest tier.

## Rationale

- The product pairs early learning with social-emotional care, so progression should reward both care and minigame activity.
- Age-appropriate accessibility and privacy requirements are product constraints, not optional polish.

## Consequences

- Heart Sparks and Star Sparks fill one 100-XP Purr-gress cycle that yields Magical Trunk rewards.
- Explorer difficulty uses large touch zones and no fail state.
- Release preparation must include COPPA, accessibility, audio, and content review work.
